Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about vehicle recovery and towing.

How fast can you reach me?
Response time depends on your exact location, traffic and the recovery situation. Send your live location on WhatsApp and we can coordinate the nearest available recovery unit.
Do you operate 24 hours?
Yes. Emergency vehicle recovery and towing assistance is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
Do you cover all seven Emirates?
Coverage includes Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. Send your location to confirm availability for your exact area.
Can you transport sports and luxury cars?
Yes. Suitable flatbed and careful loading methods can be used for sports cars, luxury cars, low-clearance vehicles and premium vehicles.
Can I contact you through WhatsApp?
Yes. WhatsApp is available on 0551525352. Send your location, vehicle type and what happened.
Can you recover accident-damaged vehicles?
Yes. Accident-damaged and disabled vehicles can be recovered and transported to a suitable garage, workshop or destination.
Do you provide winch recovery?
Yes. Winch recovery can be arranged for vehicles stuck in difficult positions or certain off-road and sand-recovery situations.
How do I request a price?
Call 0551525352 or send a WhatsApp message with your location and vehicle details so the recovery requirements can be discussed before dispatch.
